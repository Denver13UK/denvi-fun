Introducing: AMpoints
What are AMpoints?

AMpoints are virtual gaming points that you gain through in-game rewards. You can get a lot of them!

What can I do with them?

You can spend them on unlocking games and in-game items.

Is there a daily limit?

No, there is no limit. BUT, the points get reset to 0 globally every month. Why? So you can challenge and work harder to earn them again and have x2 fun!

So what's the point in them?

Well, it works like arcades! You can get MANY of them simply, spend them, and get them again!

In conclusion,

AMpoints are virtual gaming points where you can earn a lot of, and spend them for in-game rewards, or buying more games so you could enjoy them. They get reset every month, but no worries because, there are many to hold. It doesnt matter if you have the most of them! Or the least. It's all fun and games!
